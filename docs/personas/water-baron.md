# Water Baron
Path: the tap. Filtered tap water (filter + chlorine) to Basewater, the purifier to High Quality, Pure for the stockpiler; later fish tanks on the same water. Cheapest from Water Merchant; else Water Trading (300).
Buy: taps by day 10 (250, 350); a second purifier by day 20; Vending Fountain (1200) at cash 2500; Aquaculture (400) once two purifiers run.
Factions: Lower Levels 20 then 60 (rumours of water events); Upper Levels for HQ and Pure.
Never: raise a label; sell untested treated water; sell a filter, purifier or tablet as goods.
Windfall: grab purifiers, moisture farms, filters, quality modules. Stay on path unless a windfall clearly beats it.
Win: buyout by about day 65, never a fraud or endangerment crime.
