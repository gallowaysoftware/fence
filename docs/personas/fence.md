# Fence
Path: the Black Market's shop. Buy stolen cheap (it shows 10-25 % under its clean value), hide it in the bay while it cools (18 a night anywhere), sell it at heat 0. Fund it with wine and the Board: the loop is thin.
Buy: the permit (1,500) once cash covers it, rent and 500; Retired Winemaker (200); Criminal Networking (350) from favor or surplus; Clemency (250) as insurance; Inspection Early Warning (300) only if you sell warm; then favor on supplier shareholders.
Factions: Black Market 40 (a thief every week); Security above -40 (hard starts at -37.5).
Never: display or sell a hot item that can cool before rent; sell to security; report or haggle with a thief; pay persona upgrades from working cash.
Win: the buyout or the mortgage, record under 300 crime.
