# Fence
Path: the Black Market's shop. Buy stolen cheap (extra rep), hide it in the smuggler bay while it cools to heat 0 (18 a night), sell it clean (fencing crime is price × heat). Fund it with wine: the loop alone is thin.
Buy: Inspection Early Warning (300) by day 7; the permit (1,500) once cash covers it, rent and 500 (bribing it cost 3,686); Retired Winemaker (200); Criminal Networking (350) from favor or surplus; Clemency (250) as insurance; then favor on supplier shareholders.
Factions: Black Market 40 then 60; Security above -60.
Never: display or sell a hot item that can cool before rent; sell to security; pay persona upgrades from working cash.
Win: the mortgage (4-5k cash by ~day 120), record under 300 crime.
