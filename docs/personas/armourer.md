# Armourer
Path: arms the station legally. Gun orders come only to the permit in the window; modded guns, ammo, printed guns and parts, sold into weapon rises. Cheapest from Gunsmith; else Retired Gunsmith (975 on hard) + Weapon Permit I (450).
Buy: keep Permit I (Security 20) on display; Permit II (900 on hard) at Security 50, after Georgio's loan; Permit III (1 350) at Security 80, only with an SMG or shotgun to sell; furnace for ingots at cash 1500.
Factions: Security first (clean trades with officers, wanted captures); Lower Levels above 0.
Never: hold a weapon above your permit (each covers its own tier only); let Security fall under 20; buy stolen guns.
Windfall: grab printer chips, gun mods, frames, ingots.
Win: Permit III and the buyout with Security at 80.

**Reading:** the armourer's line is *order-limited, not capital-limited*: Basic clients come
0.6/day and Mid 0.5 against one gun per 4.67 printer-nights — the printers idle, the permits
don't. So the buy order is furnace+blast (7n) → Permit I (day 1, it IS the path) → Permit II
(1350, gates the heavy 134/night line, measured +76 win days... but +72 to +76 *worse* win days
in the gym — the forge LOST to the reference on the ~day-105 cash route). The measured verdict
in ARMS §6: **the forge does not pay on the Armourer's route** — 11–18 runs built a gun, kit
spend 1 500–1 800, win days +54 to +76 later. The old printer's pistols (25/night, 24n) are the
only forge line that moved win days at all (+54 vs +72). Payback arithmetic says the frames are
fine; the *orders* say no. Trust the orders: stay Permit I + pistols, bank the rest.
