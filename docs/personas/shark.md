# Shark
Path: nothing but nerve. Scavenge every night, flip whatever comes in; hard bargains with sellers who need cash, markups on buyers in a hurry; threats when the odds favour you. Rock Bottom is the native start.
Buy: Landlord Grace (100) the first day it is affordable; Seller Referral (200) by day 10; Showcase I; then whatever engine your best find points to.
Pivot: the first machine you find or win is your engine; adopt its path's order.
Factions: hard on Upper Levels and tourists; fair to Black Market (losses double) and Lower Levels.
Never: be generous for its own sake; let a faction come within 30 of -100; threaten Security.
Windfall: everything is a windfall; flip it unless it starts an engine.
Win: survive the first month, then the buyout by any means.
