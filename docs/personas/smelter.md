# Smelter
Path: trash into metal. Dumping ground every night, furnace every night (2 ore + flux, then the blast module on ore: ~85 a night at 16 power a smelt), ingots held for MATERIAL rises. Cheapest from Junker; else the inventor's furnace (200).
Buy: flux always in stock; GP Mining Coop (250) by day 10; blast module (150) by day 14; Miner shareholder (900) once ore is a weekly buy; at cash 2000 the gunsmith printer to turn ingots into gun parts.
Factions: Lower Levels 20 and 40 (rare merchant); Security neutral.
Never: leave the furnace idle a night; sell ingots into a MATERIAL fall; buy scrap above 10.
Windfall: grab furnaces, blast and junk modules, ore, turbo boosters. Stay on path unless a windfall clearly beats it.
Win: buyout by about day 75 on metal.
