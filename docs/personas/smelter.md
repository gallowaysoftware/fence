# Smelter
Path: trash into metal. Dumping ground every night, furnace every night (2 ore + flux, then the blast module on ore: ~85 a night at 16 power a smelt), ingots held for MATERIAL rises. From the Apprentice: the inventor's furnace (200).
Buy: flux always in stock; GP Mining Coop (250) by day 10; blast module (150) by day 14; Miner shareholder (900) once ore is a weekly buy; at cash 2000 the gunsmith printer to turn ingots into gun parts.
Factions: Lower Levels 20 and 40 (rare merchant); Security neutral.
Never: leave the furnace idle a night; sell ingots into a MATERIAL fall; buy scrap above 10.
Windfall: grab furnaces, blast and junk modules, ore, turbo boosters. Stay on path unless a windfall clearly beats it.
Win: buyout by about day 75 on metal.

## Payback nights (hard; cost ÷ net per night, measured + docs/re/PRODUCTION.md §4)

| Engine | Cost | Per night | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| blast module (150 → 225) | 225 | 85 − 18 = **+67** (2 ingots vs 1) | **3.4** |
| furnace (375) | 375 | 18 (2 scrap), 85 blasted | **20.8** (blasted) |
| printer (455) + modules | ~1100 | 25 (printed pistol) | 44 |
| Perfect-ingot line (cores ~4000) | ~4000 | +75 over Very high | 55+ |

**Reading:** the furnace is mid (20 nights blasted), but the *blast module* is the archetype's
second-best buy (3.4 nights) and makes the furnace itself worth owning. Ore is free from
scavenge; the miner (375) only cuts input runs. The printer is weak standalone — it exists to
serve arms orders, not to print for the counter. Perfect ingots (the cores) never pay back
inside a run: Very high is the ceiling. Savings phase: essentially never — everything worth
buying is under 400; the exception is the arms line's permits, which belong to the armourer.
