# Vintner
Path: bloomberries on Pure or reed-cured water, minerals in band; good berries to a bottle until it scores Master (three + yeast on HQ water, four on Base); wine aged and sold to Upper Levels. Cheapest from Hydroponic Farmer; else Retired Farmer (350).
Buy: Retired Winemaker (200) by day 3; Water Trading (300) by day 14; a hydroreed bay on tap water; bottles and yeast each winemaker visit; the permit at cash ~2,500; a rack (6 bottles) for Masters only.
Keep: ~2 L clean water per empty bottle; a berry that only lifts a bottle 3 to 4 sells as food.
Factions: Upper Levels 20 (+15% sales), then 100; Lower Levels above 0.
Never: refuse a buyer to age a bottle (age a buffer, off the case); fake yeast; ghost water in a bay.
Win: the mortgage (4-5k cash by ~day 120), the finest cellar.
