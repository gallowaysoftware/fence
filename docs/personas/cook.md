# Cook
Path: a drug lab. Caps extracted at 44 °C (dead from 60: never a plate overnight), dried in the evaporator, pressed to dust. Chemist start: the cartel (quota, stipend, burglary protection). Else Retired Chemist (1 500 on hard): no cartel.
Quota: 10 dust at 40% first, +5 and +5% a week (cap 70%), judged on the TRUE mean purity of all dust on the counters; cut to required + 10. None, or a mean ≤ a quarter of required, ends the run.
Buy: evaporator cells first; Wild's Fixers by day 14; Evidence Removal before evidence 80.
Factions: Cartel 25 (stipend) then 50; Black Market 40; Security > -60.
Never: pressed dust held between visits (bank the residue; press on the handler's morning); caps in the window.
Win: cartel 100 starts the exit; 200, 5+ visits later, completes it.
