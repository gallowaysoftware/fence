# Rancher
Path: livestock for the Lower Levels. Rats bred on nutrifruit and butchered near 400 g, then tilapia in tanks; meat and food held for FOOD rises. Cheapest from Rat Wrangler (free kit); else Ranching (750) or Aquaculture (400 after Water Trading 300).
Buy: Retired Farmer (350) by day 14 to grow feed; a second cage at cash 1500; serum against disease; auto butcher at cash 2500.
Factions: Lower Levels 20, 40, 100 (Pillar: upgrades 15% off).
Never: sell breeding stock; let feed or power run out; sell food into a FOOD fall when it keeps.
Windfall: grab cages, feed dispensers, tanks, butchers. Stay on path unless a windfall clearly beats it.
Win: Lower Levels 100 and the buyout by day 90.
