# Rancher
Path: livestock for the Lower Levels. Rats cut at 401 g (or their best by age 10, never later), then tilapia in tanks; meat held for FOOD rises. Cheapest from Rat Wrangler (free kit); else Ranching (750) or Aquaculture (400 after Water Trading 300).
Buy: an ingot per cage row (3 to 8) before anything; cheap bought food (ice cream, milk, canned meat); breeders by growth and immunity; a second cage at cash 1500; auto butcher at cash 2500.
Factions: Lower Levels 20, 40, 100 (Pillar: upgrades 15% off).
Never: rat meat in a rat feeder (prion); cut a breeder; let feed or the tanks' power run out; sell food into a FOOD fall when it keeps.
Windfall: grab cages, feed dispensers, tanks, butchers, ingots.
Win: Lower Levels 100 and the buyout by day 90.
