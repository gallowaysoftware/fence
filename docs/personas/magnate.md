# Magnate
Path: the generalist. Trading pays the rent; every engine that pays back within two weeks gets added: cellar first, then water, furnace, vending.
Buy: Showcase I by day 10; Retired Winemaker (200) and Retired Farmer (350) by day 14; Water Trading (300) by day 21; furnace + flux by day 28; Vending Machine (1200) at cash 3000; Renovations after that.
Pivot: add an engine only when cash after it covers two rents; drop one that misses its payback.
Factions: Lower Levels 20, Upper Levels 20, Security 40; none below 0.
Never: let a machine sit idle a night; buy an engine you cannot feed.
Windfall: grab any machine or module that fits an engine you run. Stay on path unless a windfall clearly beats it.
Win: buyout by day 60 with four engines running.
